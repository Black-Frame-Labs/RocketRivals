Privacy Policy - Rocket Rivals

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Rocket Rivals is an Android local multiplayer arcade game developed by BlackFrameLabs.

BlackFrameLabs respects your privacy and has designed Rocket Rivals to collect as little information as reasonably possible.

Rocket Rivals does not require a user account, does not contain advertising, and does not currently send gameplay analytics or crash reports to BlackFrameLabs.

Information We Do Not Ask You to Provide

Rocket Rivals does not require users to provide personal information such as:

  • Real names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Messages
  • Payment information
  • Precise location
  • User-written profile information

Player codenames shown in the game are generated locally by the game.

Rocket Rivals does not provide gameplay chat or other free-form communication between players.

Information Stored on Your Device

Rocket Rivals stores some information locally on your device so that the game can remember your progress, content and preferences.

This may include:

  • Generated player codename
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Career progression and unlocked ship rank
  • User-created arenas
  • Other local game configuration required for normal operation

This information is stored locally by the application and is not uploaded to a BlackFrameLabs account or online gameplay server.

Clearing the application's data or uninstalling Rocket Rivals normally removes locally stored application data, subject to Android device backup and restore behavior.

Nearby Multiplayer

Rocket Rivals supports local multiplayer between nearby Android devices using Google Nearby Connections and underlying nearby communication technologies such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and peer-to-peer networking.

Nearby communication is used to discover participating devices, establish local game connections and synchronize multiplayer gameplay.

Information exchanged between devices participating in the same local multiplayer session may include:

  • Generated player codenames
  • Lobby and player status information
  • Game configuration
  • Gameplay state
  • Player gameplay inputs
  • Arena information required for the current match
  • User-created arena information when a player chooses to share an arena with another participating device

This information is exchanged between nearby participating devices for multiplayer functionality.

BlackFrameLabs does not operate an online gameplay server and does not receive or store the nearby multiplayer gameplay traffic on a BlackFrameLabs server.

Nearby Device and Location Permissions

Android may require permissions related to nearby devices, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or location in order for nearby-device discovery and communication to function.

These permissions are used for local multiplayer connectivity.

Rocket Rivals does not intentionally collect, store or transmit your GPS coordinates or precise physical location to BlackFrameLabs.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

The current version of Rocket Rivals does not send gameplay analytics to BlackFrameLabs or Google Analytics.

Rocket Rivals does not currently include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or another crash-reporting SDK.

Rocket Rivals does not use an advertising identifier for analytics or advertising.

BlackFrameLabs does not use Rocket Rivals data for advertising personalization and does not sell user data.

Version and Update Checking

Rocket Rivals may periodically download a small version-information file from the official Rocket Rivals website.

This is used to:

  • Determine whether a newer version of Rocket Rivals is available
  • Inform players about available updates
  • Display version or release information

Rocket Rivals does not intentionally include personal information in this request.

As with ordinary Internet requests, the website hosting provider may receive standard technical information required to deliver the requested file, such as an IP address or HTTP request information.

Google Play

Versions of Rocket Rivals distributed through Google Play use Google Play for application distribution and updates.

Google may process information associated with Google Play installation, distribution, security and account services according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Third-Party Services and Technologies

Rocket Rivals currently relies on third-party services or technologies including:

  • Google Nearby Connections - for local nearby-device multiplayer connectivity
  • GitHub Pages - for hosting the Rocket Rivals website, privacy policy and version/update information
  • Google Play - for distribution and updates of Google Play releases

These third parties may process technical information necessary to provide their services according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

BlackFrameLabs does not sell personal information.

Rocket Rivals does not currently send gameplay analytics or crash reports to BlackFrameLabs.

During nearby multiplayer, information required for the game is exchanged with the other devices participating in that local multiplayer session.

Information may also be processed by third-party service providers when technically necessary to provide services such as nearby-device connectivity, website hosting and Google Play distribution.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game settings, progression, generated player information and user-created arenas are stored locally on the user's device.

Local application information generally remains on the device until it is changed or removed through application functionality, the application's data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Because Rocket Rivals does not require an account and BlackFrameLabs does not operate an online gameplay database containing this local game data, BlackFrameLabs generally cannot remotely access or delete information stored only on a player's device.

For privacy or deletion questions concerning information that may have been received by BlackFrameLabs, contact: blackframelabs.dev@gmail.com.

Data Security

Rocket Rivals is designed to minimize the collection and transmission of user information.

Internet communication used for version checking uses HTTPS.

Nearby multiplayer communication is handled using Google Nearby Connections and communication technologies provided by Android and the participating devices.

No method of electronic communication or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Rocket Rivals is designed to minimize the collection of personal information, including when used by younger players.

Rocket Rivals does not require accounts, real names, email addresses or other user profile information and does not provide gameplay chat.

Rocket Rivals does not contain advertising and does not intentionally collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling.

If you believe that personal information from a child has been unintentionally received by BlackFrameLabs, please contact us at blackframelabs.dev@gmail.com so that the matter can be investigated and, where applicable, the information deleted.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may provide rights concerning personal information, including rights of access, correction or deletion.

Rocket Rivals does not use player accounts, and most game information is stored only on the player's device. As a result, BlackFrameLabs may not possess information that allows us to identify a particular player or associate local game information with a specific real-world person.

Privacy inquiries can be sent to blackframelabs.dev@gmail.com.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

BlackFrameLabs may update this Privacy Policy when Rocket Rivals, its data practices or applicable requirements change.

The current version will be published on the official Rocket Rivals website with an updated revision date.
